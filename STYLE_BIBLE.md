# Style Bible — Hindi Devotional-Moral Kids' Cartoon

Derived from a visual study of the reference video "गणेश जी के साथ जगन्नाथ और बलराम की मस्ती!" on the Jay Jagannath channel (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SYkEN_3n3PI). Used for style direction only. Do not reuse that channel's characters, deity designs, logo, music or stories.

## What was studied, and its limits

- The video page blocked automated fetching, so the study was done by playing the video in a browser and sampling about ten frames over roughly 40 seconds starting near the 11:41 timestamp that was linked, plus the page title, description and the channel's recommended-video thumbnails.
- Audio could not be heard. Voice, accent and lipsync notes below come from the on-screen Hindi captions, mouth shapes and the Hindi description, not from listening.
- Only a short stretch of a long episode was seen, so story structure notes are inferred from the description and thumbnails and are marked as such.

## Observed visual traits

| Area | Observation | How the agent reproduces it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rendering | Flat-cel 2D Indian TV animation, clean colour fills, light shading, soft dark outlines. | "2D flat-cel Indian kids' TV cartoon, soft rounded dark-brown outlines" in every image prompt |
| Child characters | Chibi proportions: head about half the body, huge round glossy black eyes with white highlights, tiny nose, small mouth, rosy cheeks, ornate gold crowns and jewellery. | Chibi wording and a fixed look per child character |
| Adult characters | Taller, slimmer, normal-proportioned adults with soft faces, saree and jewellery detail, calmer expressions. | Elders and adults are not chibi; keep faces gentle |
| Animals and creatures | A giant stylised bird with layered feathers, a fruit basket, simple props. Creatures are stylised and friendly. | Optional animal companion with big eyes; keep it non-speaking unless needed |
| Backgrounds | Pastel painted worlds: sky gradients with clouds, big stone pillars, pink-purple rock formations, snow-covered blue mountains, blossoming bushes. Strong depth, bright daylight. | Environment reference per location and fixed layout text |
| Framing | Mostly medium two-shots and group shots, cutting to a close-up of one speaking face; occasional wide establishing shots. Calm, mostly locked camera. | Two-shot openers, solo close-ups for each spoken line |
| Animation | Limited animation: blinks, head tilts, small hand movements, mouth opening and closing with speech. | Prompts ask for gentle actions and mouth moving with the words |
| Subtitles | Hindi Devanagari subtitles, white with dark outline, bottom centre, one or two lines. | Added only in the editor as a text layer |
| Watermark | Small channel logo in the top-right corner. | Optional text watermark with the user's own channel name, added in the editor only |
| Language | Simple, warm, colloquial Hindi, clear child-friendly sentences, moral explained in dialogue. | Short Devanagari lines, one speaker per clip |
| Tone | Devotional, affectionate, family values, gentle humour, mischief between children. | Moral-story structure, light comic relief, respectful treatment |
| Episode shape (inferred from the description) | A question or problem sends children on a small journey, with a lesson about care, family or kindness. | Hook → trouble → effort → turning moment → resolution → moral → final gag |

## Palette and prompt phrases

- Base: sky blue, lavender, soft pink, marigold orange, temple gold, grass green, warm cream.
- Outline: dark brown, even thickness, slightly thicker on the silhouette.
- Shading: single soft shadow tone, bright even lighting.
- Phrases that work: "2D flat-cel Indian kids' TV cartoon", "soft rounded dark-brown outlines", "chibi child with huge round glossy black eyes and white highlights", "gentle friendly face", "pastel painted background", "fluffy clouds", "bright cheerful lighting", "gold jewellery detail".
- Phrases to avoid: stickman, 3D, Pixar, photorealistic, grotesque caricature, "text", "caption", "subtitle", "logo", "watermark".

## Character design checklist

Each character needs: a unique silhouette, hair shape and colour, an outfit colour that no other main character shares, one memorable prop or trait and a fixed voice description (child, elder, proud, kind; speed and pitch). Children are chibi; elders and adults are slim. Keep outfit colours constant for the whole video.

## Lipsync notes

- VideoExpress Advanced Mode generates the speech and the mouth movement from the prompt line "<Name> says in Hindi: <line>". Lipsync HD is not used.
- Chibi characters have tiny mouths, so ask for "mouth opens and closes clearly in time with the words" and use a mid or close shot so the movement is readable.
- Non-speakers in a two-shot tend to move their mouths too. Use a solo shot of the speaker for each line.
- Lines over 40 Devanagari characters are often cut off at clip end. Add two seconds or shorten the line.

## Set layout rule

Write each recurring set once as a short list of landmark → screen side → distance, and paste it unchanged into every prompt. See the four sets in examples/story-mitti-ki-ghanti.md.
