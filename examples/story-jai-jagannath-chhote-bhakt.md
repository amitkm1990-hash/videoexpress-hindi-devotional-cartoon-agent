# "जय जगन्नाथ | रथ यात्रा में छोटे भक्त का चमत्कार"

Original story and script. 46 clips of about 8 seconds, about 6 minutes after trimming, 16:9, Hindi. Type D means the character speaks on screen (Advanced Mode, one speaker per clip). Type N means a narrator voice-over cutaway by Nani Maa (Narration Video route; if no Hindi-capable voice exists, make it a D line spoken by Nani Maa to camera).

**Moral:** रथ सबके हाथों से चलता है. A chariot moves by many hearts pulling together, and no one is too small to serve.

**Story shape:** hook (Rath Yatra day) → trouble (proud wrestler pushes the children back) → effort (Kanu and Munni bring water, goat gag) → low point (the wrestler pulls alone and the wheel sticks in the sand) → turning moment (Kanu leads the chant, thousands pull in rhythm) → change of heart (the wrestler humbled) → moral → final gag (Lali the goat eats the wrestler's garland).

## Respectful depiction rule for this story

The deities are never drawn as characters and never speak. Show devotion through symbols only: the tall decorated chariot with its canopy curtains closed, flags, conch sounds, golden light, marigold petals and the devotees' faces. Do not draw faces or idols inside the chariot. No joke touches the chariot, the deities or the rituals; humour comes only from Kanu, Munni, the goat and the wrestler.
