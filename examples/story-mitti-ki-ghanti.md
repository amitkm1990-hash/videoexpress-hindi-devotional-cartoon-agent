# "मिट्टी की घंटी" (The Clay Bell)

Original story and script. 46 clips of about 8 seconds, about 6 minutes after trimming, 16:9, Hindi. Type D means the character speaks on screen (Advanced Mode, one speaker per clip). Type N means a narrator voice-over cutaway by Dadi Maa (Narration Video route; if no Hindi-capable voice exists, make it a D line spoken by Dadi Maa to camera).

**Moral:** भगवान को सोना नहीं, सच्चा मन चाहिए. God hears a sincere heart, not wealth.

**Story shape:** hook (silent bell) → trouble (gold tongue costs a lakh) → effort (Bholu makes a clay bell, messy gags, shared roti) → low point (Seth mocks it, the gold bell sounds dull) → turning moment (Bholu rings the clay bell, the golden bell rings by itself) → change of heart (Seth builds a school without his name) → moral → final gag (Moti steals a laddu).
